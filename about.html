3-Layer Quality Control

Every order goes through rigorous inspection at three stages.

Layer 1: Material Inspection

Incoming SBS paperboard tested for brightness (90+ GT), density, caliper, and food-contact compliance before production begins.

Layer 2: In-Line Inspection

Printing alignment, color accuracy, and structural integrity checked at every production stage by dedicated QC engineers.

Layer 3: Pre-Shipment Inspection

Final AQL 2.5 sampling inspection on finished goods — dimensions, print quality, assembly, and packaging before dispatch.
