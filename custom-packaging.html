Custom Packaging Process — From Idea to Delivery

A transparent, step-by-step process designed for B2B buyers. Low barrier to start, clear timelines at every stage, and dedicated support throughout.

Step 1

Inquiry & Consultation

You provide: Product dimensions, quantity estimate, design files (if available)

We do: Review requirements, recommend materials & structure, provide pricing

Timeline: Quote within 24 hours

Deliverable: Detailed quotation with material specs and pricing

Step 2

Dieline & Structural Design

You provide: Confirmation to proceed, product photos or samples

We do: Create custom dieline, structural drawings, 3D mockup

Timeline: 1–2 business days

Deliverable: Digital dieline (AI/PDF) for your review

Step 3

Physical Sample Production

You provide: Dieline approval, artwork files for printing

We do: Produce physical sample with your design, apply finishes

Timeline: 5–7 working days

Deliverable: Physical sample shipped to your address

Step 4

Mass Production

You provide: Sample approval, 50% deposit

We do: Mass production with 3-layer QC, printing, die-cutting, assembly

Timeline: 20–30 working days (varies by quantity & complexity)

Deliverable: Finished boxes ready for shipment

Step 5

QC & Shipment

You provide: 50% balance payment

We do: Pre-shipment AQL 2.5 inspection, packing, customs clearance, shipping

Timeline: 3–5 working days for QC & packing + transit time

Deliverable: Door-to-door delivery with tracking
