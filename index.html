Solid Bleached Sulfate (SBS) Paperboard

Our premium SBS paperboard delivers superior print sharpness, structural rigidity, and food-contact safety. The bright white surface maximizes ink vibrancy for premium brand presentation.

  • Brightness 90+ GT — brilliant white surface for vivid print reproduction
  • Density 0.82 g/cm³ — stiff, rigid structure for premium feel
  • Caliper 250–450 gsm — versatile weights for any box format
  • FDA 21 CFR compliant — direct food contact approved
